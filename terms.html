1. Educational Programs & Admissions

We provide authentic Islamic and Quranic education, including Hifz-ul-Quran (memorization), Nazra Quran with Tajweed, foundational Islamic studies (Dars-e-Nizami), Arabic language, and one-on-one virtual classes for local and international students. Admissions are granted based on evaluation, seat availability, and agreement with our institutional code of discipline.

2. Free Education Guarantee for Deserving Students

In accordance with the founding vision of Hazrat Maulana Mufti Riaz Mahmood (RH) and Islamic teachings, no deserving, orphan, or Zakat-eligible student will ever be denied Quranic education due to financial hardship. 100% free tuition, books, and hostel boarding/meals are provided to eligible Hifz students through our community Zakat and welfare fund.

3. Virtual Classroom Etiquette & Guidelines

  • Punctuality: Students must log in at least 5 minutes prior to the scheduled class time.
  • Environment: Lessons must be attended from a quiet, well-lit study room free of distractions, background conversations, and music.
  • Islamic Attire: Both students and present guardians must maintain modest, dignified Islamic clothing during live video interactions.
  • Pronunciation Observation: Video feed must be enabled during Tajweed sessions so the Asatizah can observe accurate lip articulation and pronunciation (Makharij).
  • Anti-Recording Prohibition: Unauthorized screen recording, audio/video capture, or publishing of classroom sessions, Asatizah, or fellow students on social media is strictly prohibited under Islamic principles of Pardah and institutional confidentiality.

4. Attendance & Rescheduling Policy

  • 85% Attendance: Consistent daily attendance of at least 85% is required to remain in good academic standing and qualify for formal certification or Sanad.
  • Advance Leave Notice: In case of illness or unavoidable emergency, parents must notify the administration or teacher at least 6 hours in advance via WhatsApp or email.
  • Make-up Sessions: Legitimate absences with advance notice will be rescheduled within the same calendar month, subject to teacher availability. Unannounced absences will not be made up.

5. Student Code of Conduct & Moral Tarbiyat

Students must maintain high moral character (Akhlaq), respectful conduct toward Asatizah and fellow classmates, and deep reverence for the Holy Quran. Any insubordination, offensive language, or disruption of classes may result in disciplinary action or termination of enrollment.

6. Tuition Fees, Contributions & Hadiya

For paying and overseas students, monthly tuition fees (Hadiya) contribute toward instructor honoraria, utilities, and educational facilities. Monthly fees must be paid by the 10th of each calendar month. Tuition fees for started sessions are non-refundable. Official receipts are issued for all payments and donations.
