Introduction to Madrassa

Building knowledge, practice, and character in the light of Quran & Sunnah

Madrassa Riaz ul Quran Wal Sunnah is a religious, educational, and training institution founded in 1993 by Hazrat Maulana Mufti Riaz Mahmood (RH). Since its inception, the institution has been continuously serving to promote pure Quran and Sunnah education, correct beliefs, high morals, and excellent religious upbringing.
