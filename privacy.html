Privacy Policy

Madrassa Riaz ul Quran wal Sunnah • Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Website: mrqs.pk

At Madrassa Riaz ul Quran wal Sunnah ("MRQS", "we", "our", or "us"), protecting the privacy and personal details of our students, parents, guardians, donors, and website visitors is an Amanah (a sacred, uncompromised Islamic trust). This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how it is used strictly for educational purposes, and how your data is safeguarded.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum personal details necessary to process admissions, deliver Quranic instruction, and communicate academic progress:

  • Student Particulars: Legal name, age or date of birth, gender, preferred language of instruction, prior recitation level, and selected curriculum (Hifz, Nazra, Tajweed, or Tarbiyat).
  • Parent/Guardian Information: Parent or guardian's full name, email address, mobile phone number, WhatsApp contact number, and country/city of residence.
  • Academic Progress Records: Attendance history, daily recitation logs (Sabaq, Sabqi, Manzil), Tajweed evaluation scores, and teacher comments.
  • Donation and Fee Transaction Records: Bank transfer reference numbers, deposit screenshot receipts, and transaction dates. Note: We never collect, process, or store credit/debit card numbers or bank PINs on our servers.

2. Strict Educational Use & Zero Commercialization Guarantee

We strictly guarantee that student and parent data is NEVER sold, rented, leased, traded, or shared with third-party advertisers, data brokers, or commercial entities under any circumstances.

All personal information submitted via our admission forms, contact forms, or live chat is used exclusively for:

  • Scheduling and conducting online and campus Quran classes.
  • Sending monthly academic progress reports, Tajweed evaluations, and attendance alerts to parents.
  • Issuing official admission confirmations, student registration IDs, and fee/donation receipts.
  • Responding promptly to parental inquiries and support requests.

3. Security Architecture & Technical Safeguards

We implement modern technical measures to protect all stored and transmitted data:

  • HTTPS / TLS 1.3: All communications between your browser and mrqs.pk are protected with modern transport layer encryption.
  • Cloudflare Edge Defense: Web Application Firewall (WAF), automated DDoS mitigation, and strict HTTP security response headers.
  • Web3Forms Encryption: Contact and admission form submissions pass through encrypted API pipelines with honeypot spam protection.
  • Google Firebase Realtime Database Security: Administrative data is hosted on Google Cloud infrastructure protected by strict Firebase Realtime Database security rules forbidding unauthorized public access.

4. Children's Privacy

A substantial number of our students are minors under 18 years of age. All admission applications and communications for minor students must be initiated, authorized, and supervised by a parent or legal guardian. We never publish photos or recitation videos of students on social media without express written guardian consent.

5. Minimal Cookies & Local Storage

We do not use tracking or behavioral advertising cookies. We only use functional session storage for necessary features, such as saving your language preference (English/Urdu), anti-spam verification on forms, and the customer support live chat widget (Tawk.to).

6. Your Data Rights

Parents and adult students have full rights to inspect their academic records, request corrections to contact details, or request account erasure upon completion or withdrawal from courses. Inquiries may be emailed to info@mrqs.pk.

7. Policy Updates & Official Contact
